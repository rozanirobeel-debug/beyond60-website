# Obsidian Quality Gates

A client website is not deployment-ready until it passes these gates.

## Business
- The primary audience, offer, qualified lead and conversion action are explicit.
- Every major section supports a customer question, objection or decision.
- Analytics measure qualified actions rather than traffic alone.

## Experience
- Keyboard navigation and visible focus states work.
- Reduced-motion mode remains understandable and complete.
- Mobile content order and interactions are deliberately designed.
- Animation communicates hierarchy, causality, progress or feedback.

## Performance
- Target Lighthouse performance, accessibility, best-practices and SEO scores: 90+.
- 3D and large media are lazy-loaded and have static fallbacks.
- Layout shifts are prevented and critical text is never delayed for effects.

## Ownership and safety
- Source, domains, analytics and deployment accounts remain portable.
- Secrets stay in environment variables and never enter source control.
- Forms include spam protection, validation, consent and a tested destination.
- Deployment requires human approval.
